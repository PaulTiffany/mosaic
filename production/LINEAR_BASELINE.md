# Locked baseline: one-direction lyric side-scroller

**Creative instruction (November 7, 2026).** The baseline is a **linear visual story, read from left to right exactly in lyric order**. The cathedral and visual material are subsequent presentation layers. The story must work as a silent, flat, horizontally scrolling strip *before* any 3D work.

## What must be true

1. **X is time.** Later lyrics are ALWAYS farther right on the same completed mural. No U-turns, serpentine layout, vertical registers with reversed direction, repeated visits, or search for relevant content. For sample anchors, x progresses monotonically from 0.02 to 0.98 across 276.56 seconds.
2. **One continuous horizontal picture.** A very wide, completely static glass/ceramic mural is a narrative frieze. Its successive environments transition organically: individual work → coordinated workers → abundant market → homes → fields → labor pathways → civic square → research → verification → final image. Not pasted icons and floating charts. The imagery is designed for the lyrics' order rather than shuffled interview topics.
3. **Lyrics determine the storyboard.** For each phrase record (start/end time, lyric, precise visual beat, target X, optional framing Y, source interview reference). Do this before drawing. Preserve the exact lyrical subject and emotional perspective, not just the abstract theme.
4. **The visual story unfolds only through the moving camera.** Static single image on one flat plane, stationary cathedral geometry, constant illumination. The camera tracks LEFT → RIGHT at a music-sensitive speed. Hold the current view when needed, but do not reverse. Optional small vertical tilt/height adjustment and dolly for legibility must not change the one-way reading sequence.
5. **Choruses move forward too.** Each repetition has NEW imagery at a later X position (possibly an evolving neighborhood of the same city), never a jump back or reuse of an earlier location. The last chorus is a concluding chapter located toward the far right of the artwork.
6. **Physical 3D camera.** Mount the one image as a UV texture on a planar mural wall in a large static cathedral. Actual lateral dolly creates natural perspective in the cathedral (columns, floor, ceiling). No moving world elements, no screen-space fake parallax, no crossfades.
7. **Ending.** Once camera reaches the final right-hand lyric scene, it can pull back/rotate to reveal the enormous completed mural in architectural context. This is a reveal by camera alone, NOT movement backwards along the story or an image change.
8. **Baseline first.** Verify with a lyric-timed flat side-scrolling 2D animatic from the final continuous mural before proceeding to a 3D cathedral test. That animatic is a *camera framing test*, not the final cathedral render. Do not claim final render until actually made.

## Design feasibility

The **flat panoramic mural** is the deliverable. A complete mural may need offline assembly of several art-generation passes into one visually unified immutable texture with a documented source atlas; that is different from animation. A low-resolution model image must not be sold as 16k detail simply by upscaling. Establish actual available texture resolution and maximum close-up scale before committing the final camera distance.
