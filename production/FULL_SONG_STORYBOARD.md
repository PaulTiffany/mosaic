# MOSAIC — The entire song as ONE storyboard

**106 sung lyric lines. 12 instrumental/wordless intervals. One continuous, completely fixed panoramic artwork. One camera moving left → right.**

**This is the creative master, not a selection of thematic prompts.** Each lyric has an exact location in song time, a position along the **39-meter-long mural**, and the concrete image that should be present when the moving camera reaches it. The picture exists fully formed for the whole song; no pictured object, window, tile, light or character animates.

## The creative contract

1. The next sung line reveals the next piece of visual narrative to the RIGHT. Camera X never decreases.
2. The image at each time must *depict that line*, not merely an abstract topic associated with the interview.
3. No shot requires a cut, jump or reset. Artist composes connecting geography, roads, arches, room boundaries, hands, and architectural motifs continuously from left to right.
4. Different choruses depict different scenes at NEW mural locations, carrying the question toward the final aspirational answer.
5. An instrumental break is still a camera move or hold through the completed mural, not extra animation.
6. First produce and inspect a **single global sketch of ALL 106 beats together** (a deliberately rough but coherent frieze/storyboard). Only then render detailed plates. This supersedes the earlier instruction to generate the first three unrelated finished plates as the immediate first deliverable.
7. The stained glass, mosaic, borders, cathedral architecture, and materials are static. The castle-like/medieval visual grammar is artistic; no inference of historical fact.
8. Source times below are SUNO's *best-effort* word-level alignment, not yet manually verified against a WAV. They are precise recorded model output, not necessarily ground-truth singing.

## Spatial assembly — one picture, not 106 unrelated frames

The 106 rows are **visual beats** nested in thirteen successive art production regions, not 106 jump cuts. Every art segment must read as a continuous landscape with the neighbor and contain its particular lyrics' concrete beats.

The master must be art-directed as a **very long medieval narrative frieze**; for example, one persistent horizontal walking road links the artisan's workspace → town → organization → three distinct window districts → farm → uncertain work → civic square → researcher's library → source/verification → final windows → concluding tessera. Material and architectural transitions may introduce stained glass, stone traceries and gold-grouted mosaics. These accents do not supersede the lyric sequence.

**Before any fine-art render:** assemble one low-detail full-width storyboard drawing featuring every lyrical image in its real next-eastern position. It need not be photorealistic or beautifully tiled. It needs to prove that moving a camera steadily right reveals the right visual in the right order with no revisits or gaps.

Then commission/edit the 13 final art regions, enforce source/style/boundary continuity, and assemble an immutable master texture. Check the full 2D horizontal camera test against the song. Last, transfer the static image to a flat planar surface in a photoreal static cathedral for the real moving 3D camera.

## Physical/delivery constraints

- Artist-target format: thirteen native 3072×2048 RGB still plates arranged on one 39m×2m 3D wall. The dimensions are *targets only*. Lower native resolution must be disclosed; fake upscaling does not supply detail.
- Frame is the **camera's view** of the single static picture at a song moment. The piece itself has no local animation or musical on/off effects.
- The final camera withdraws at the end to reveal the cathedral and whole immobile work.
