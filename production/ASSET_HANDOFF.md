# MOSAIC — Asset generation handoff / GPT Plan
**Deliverables are still images, not video. All material remains fixed in final render.**

## Why this exists
The film must reveal a *linear sequence of specific lyric-driven events*. A generic collage of research, factories, and AI fails. Artists/agents must build sequential visual scenes from lyrics BEFORE any cathedral/camera work. This is a single wide logical mural, not independent disconnected illustrations. The prompts below can be given to a separate GPT Plan/image provider. Approval of each scene is separate from exporting to the master.

## Immutable creative constraints
- 13 contiguous **3:2 landscape** plates, each target **3072×2048 px actual native detailed source art**, assembled as **39936×2048 px** (39 m × 2 m in the 3D scene, scaled 1:1000 px per m roughly). These are targets: if provider supplies lower native resolution, record it honestly—resampling does not recreate detail.
- Absolute left-to-right progression in Suno take 1 order (the pan reaches the last panel by 4:28; final seconds are camera pullback).
- Each plate must be perfectly front-facing, RECTILINEAR and flat, no picture frame, cathedral room, border, faux perspective or external spectators baked into image. The cathedral is separate *static real 3D geometry*.
- Same coherent mosaic craft across all plates: real ceramic and glass tessera, visible lead/grout, cobalt/ultramarine/teal/amber, warm gold and selective garnet; believable artisanship, not pixel art or icons.
- The main horizontal narrative path and horizon MUST connect at exact left/right edges of adjacent plates. If not, revise the scene asset rather than disguising discontinuity with a random fade.
- Story content is static: different moments shown along **space**, not time. Nothing in the picture moves. Stained glass at chorus windows may be included in the art texture, or as optional separate static cathedral-mounted sections LATER; it is not required for baseline.
- Use SAME repeat characters for: researcher, individual maker, worker, and ongoing city; visually distinguish each person. Never paste a giant AI face in center.
- Prefer a panoramic painted procession / medieval narrative relief grammar. Distinct physical settings flow through roads, doors, rivers, walls, buildings; no unrelated floating clusters.
- The existing album cover may inform palette/material, but NOT composition or page layout. Earlier image generation attempts were rejected.
- Record source, generation model, prompt, actual dimensions, file hash and cost for every asset. No invented acceptance.
- For native text that must be exact (e.g., `40%`, `2%` or bibliographic marks), add a separately documented flat compositor layer into the still *before* freezing final texture; do not pretend generated tiny lettering is accurate.

## Naming and assembly
Save files exactly as `assets/incoming/00-one-little-piece.png` through `assets/incoming/12-outro-and-seam.png`. Every image must have identical dimension 3072×2048 and RGB/RGBA (no animation or transparency requirement). The adjoining edges should be artist-checked at 100%. The production stitcher will FAIL rather than silently deform/crop mismatched files. The source plates remain individually inspectable and the assembled deliverable is **ONE immutable mural master**.

### Global continuity reminder (include in EVERY provider task)
> This image is one consecutive chapter of a completed, unmoving, physically fabricated mosaic frieze. Generate an orthographic, full-bleed portion of ONE continuous narrative, with EXACTLY the next described lyrics' imagery, not a montage of all song topics. Scene transition is spatial along the horizontal X axis. Keep a continuous cobblestone road/horizon near the lower middle of the picture, consistent craft material, scale, illumination and adjoining edge scenery. The next frame's left edge will physically touch this frame's right edge.
