# Lyric-locked mural composition / cinematic pilgrimage
**Creative correction (November 7, 2026):** Build the IMAGE FROM THE LYRICS AND CAMERA JOURNEY, not the camera route from a collage of interview topics.

The mural is one **finished flat continuous tessellated artwork**, UV-mapped to one planar surface inside a static photoreal cathedral. Everything depicted already exists. Neither painted windows nor people, machines, wheat, tiles, light, text nor scenery animate. **Only a real 3D camera travels**. Every reveal happens by composition and framing.

**Order of operations:** 1) lyric-timing map → 2) spatial story atlas → 3) single master mural → 4) actual 3D cathedral plane → 5) camera route. The previous generated image (faces, charts, farm, factory, researcher thrown into a picturesque collage) is **rejected**, not a master and not a reference for layout.

## Composition: three interconnected registers, single picture

Think of one continuous illuminated manuscript (not panels, not a grid of slides) with a winding visual river/street/road that naturally leads the eye along a serpentine, single-camera route:

- **Upper register, left → right (0:00–1:32):** fragment → individuals → complementary organization → automated productivity → abundance → question of purchasing power in city windows. A **single continuous city-and-human landscape** grows around these events; at the windows it is possible to inspect lives, not a generic skyline.
- **Middle register, right → left (1:32–3:07):** abundant harvest despite declining economic share → markets and buying power → divergent wages and uncertain new doors → windows revisited from another physical region of the SAME contiguous city → civic agency and political constraints. The city can extend across registers via a winding street, a river, bridges and interlocking arched motifs.
- **Lower register, left → right (3:07–3:45):** individual scholar entering unfamiliar disciplines → cross-field collaboration → testing sources and inspecting tile joins → human author still shaping the argument. The mosaic seam is literally legible in the camera's close approach.
- **Final passage (3:45–4:36):** pull away through photoreal cathedral space; the fixed, pre-existing city, farms, organizations, civic institutions and research all become one coherent monumental picture. Last lyric ends with a focus on *the real physical seams* visible from a camera-only perspective, not an animated light effect.

The palette and material continuity derive from our commissioned MOSAIC cover (indigo/cobalt, warm amber/gold, magenta glass, visible grout); do NOT paste the cover into the wall. Do NOT add cinematic human silhouettes in front of the image into the mural texture itself. Any physical architectural stone borders live in the cathedral geometry, outside the flat art plane.

## Camera and mural production contracts

- One UV image, one plane, one fixed scene. Avoid baking cathedral architecture, the floor, or the observers standing before it into the mural itself.
- Design transitions so the camera can follow an uninterrupted path with no teleport: upper-right windows → right-middle farm; middle-left civic scene → left-lower researcher; final dolly outward.
- The two chorus visits must **not** require the camera to jump across the picture; place meaningful economic-access imagery at both route locations as two neighborhoods of one city.
- Preserve negative space and recognizability at every intended lens magnification; a 1–2k image blown to 16k cannot create true fine-grained readability.
- Treat diagram labels (40%, 2%) as details to verify after image creation. If tiny generated typography is corrupted, replace the text via a documented, consistent artwork compositing pass before final texture freeze.
- The full mural composition is an artistic interpretation, not a photographic representation of interview guests.
- The film will ultimately credit the original interview, actual generative tools/technology authors, and source provenance. No outside assets should be passed off as ours.

## Before a second image generation

Do **not** prompt “create a cinematic cathedral mosaic of AI, farms, science, and markets.” That produces a *thematic collage* and fails the user's demand.

The correct prompt must specify the **exact integrated wide composition / route / scene order / adjacency** above. First create a **flat orthographic mural master without cathedral, perspective, frame, watchers or architecture**. Validate it by simulating the camera's lyric-timed inspection as still-image crops. Only after it passes should it be mounted inside a cathedral.
